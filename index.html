About Patricia

Patricia Arnaiz Castro is an Associate Professor at the University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (ULPGC), in the Department of Subject-Specific Pedagogy. Her teaching focuses on English language teacher education, while her research addresses foreign language learning, affective factors and bilingual education.
